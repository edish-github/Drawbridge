# NimbusWrite AI — AI Data Use Policy

**Synthetic document. Generated for the Drawbridge project.**

Version 1.6 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Training

Customer content submitted on the enterprise tier is not used to train or fine-tune our own
models.

Customer content is transmitted to our model provider for inference. The terms governing that
provider's use of transmitted content are set out in our agreement with them, which is
available under NDA.

## 2. Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Submitted text | 30 days |
| Generated outputs | 30 days |
| Prompt and completion logs | 90 days |

Logs are accessible to the support engineering team for debugging.

## 3. Output controls

Generated outputs pass through a safety filter before being returned. Outputs are not reviewed
by a human unless a customer raises a support ticket referencing them.

## 4. Human review

Support engineers may read submitted text and generated outputs when investigating a ticket.
Access is logged.
